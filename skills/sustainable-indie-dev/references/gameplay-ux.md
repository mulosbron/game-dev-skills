# Gameplay & UX

## Controls Are Everything

Controls define the game experience. Choose genre-appropriate, fluid control schemes over complex ones that feel unnatural. A game with simple but responsive controls beats a deep game with clunky input every time.

## Difficulty & Flow

- **Too easy:** Add threats, consequences, or time pressure.
- **Too hard:** Teach the player before testing them. Scaffold difficulty.
- **UI clarity:** A clean, organized inventory and interface makes the game feel "finished" and professional.

## Feedback (Juice)

Make every action feel consequential through visual and audio feedback:
- Screen shake (short, decaying — 50-200ms)
- Hit effects and impact sounds
- Particle bursts, flashes, scale tweens

A simple mechanic with good feedback feels better than a complex mechanic with none.

## Customization Options

Give players control over their experience:
- Grid/scale settings
- Screen shake intensity
- Control remapping
- Difficulty modifiers

## World Generation

Repetitive structures bore players. Use:
- Varied biomes with distinct rules
- Complex generation algorithms that avoid obvious patterns
- Hand-crafted landmarks within procedural worlds
