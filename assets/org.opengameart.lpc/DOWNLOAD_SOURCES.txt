LIBERATED PIXEL CUP (LPC) - MASTER ECOSYSTEM SOURCES
================================================================================

The Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) is a master collaborative pixel art ecosystem
providing 16x16 / 32x32 / 64x64 spritesheets, tilesets, and modular characters.

Official Sources & Repositories:
- OpenGameArt LPC Master: https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-collection
- Universal LPC Character Generator (89,000+ pieces):
  https://github.com/universal-lpc-spritesheet-character-generator/universal-lpc-spritesheet-character-generator
- LPC Revised 4-Seasons Terrain & Biomes:
  https://github.com/ElizaWy/LPC
- LPC Tools (Slicing & Recoloring):
  https://github.com/bluecarrot16/lpctools

Assembly Commands:
Use `python tools/lpc_character_builder.py` to assemble modular characters and
generate horizontal GameMaker `_stripN.png` animations with legal credits.
